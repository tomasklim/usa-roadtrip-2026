Charging

A Model Y Long Range realistically does 250–280 miles at highway speed, less in the mountains and in the cold. Highlighted rows need active management — and one of them is genuinely critical.

Northern Yellowstone — the one real risk

Gardiner has no Supercharger — only an L2 at Yellowstone Forever (2 ports) and a CCS L3 at the Gardiner Sinclair. Without a charge, the stretch from the Bozeman Supercharger through Gardiner, Lamar Valley and the whole park loop to the West Yellowstone Supercharger is over 300 miles on one battery, and that does not work. There are two fixes and you want both:

  • book a Gardiner motel with an L2 charger — eight hours overnight adds roughly 240 miles;
  • ask the Turo host for the CCS Combo 1 adapter as a backup.
